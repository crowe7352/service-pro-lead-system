FAQ

Common questions before we talk

How fast will I see results?

Most clients start seeing qualified leads within the first 30 days. Full system results — optimized cost-per-lead and consistent pipeline — typically materialize within 60–90 days as we gather data and refine targeting.

Do I need a big budget to get started?

We work with businesses who are serious about growth and have the capacity to handle it. We'll discuss what ad spend makes sense for your goals during the strategy call. There's no minimum ad budget requirement to have the conversation.

How is this different from Angi, HomeAdvisor, or Thumbtack?

On those platforms, you're competing against 5–10 other contractors for the same lead. We build you an exclusive lead system — every lead generated is yours alone. We also focus on higher-ticket jobs, not just the cheapest quote-seekers.

What if I'm not tech-savvy?

This is completely done for you. We set up, manage, and optimize everything — ads, CRM, follow-up sequences, landing pages. You focus on running your business. We handle the marketing machine.

Is there a long-term contract?

We offer month-to-month and 6-month agreements depending on your goals. That said, we invest a significant amount of setup time in the first 30 days, so the businesses that see the best results commit to at least 90 days of optimization.
